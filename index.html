About the Astrologer

Astro Khewan Sharma

Astro Khewan Sharma Jyotish Kendra offers guidance rooted in the Vedic astrology tradition (Jyotish), where the positions of the Navagrahas are studied to better understand life's journey.

Every consultation is approached with respect, patience and a sincere effort to listen. Astrology is offered as guidance and reflection, not as a guarantee of outcomes.
